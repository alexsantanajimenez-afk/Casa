How it works

Four steps, one single owner.

Order matters: that is why we review the documents before the goods leave.

  1. Pick-up and reviewWe check invoice, data and specific documentation.
  2. Declaration and clearanceWe file the DUA and handle the clearance.
  3. TransitThe goods travel with the clearance already coordinated.
  4. Delivery and closingDelivery at destination and a complete file.

Frequently asked questions

Before shipping.

Answer first, detail after.

Do I need customs clearance to ship to the Canary Islands?

Generally yes: the Canary Islands have their own tax and customs regime, and the goods are cleared and declared. We confirm what applies to your shipment. wording to validate with customs

What is the DUA and who files it?

The DUA is the declaration used to clear goods. We prepare and file it ourselves, as customs agents.

Can you carry dangerous goods?

Yes. We carry IMO cargo by sea and dangerous goods by other modes. We review classification and documentation before accepting the shipment. accepted classes to be confirmed

Do you advise companies starting to export?

Yes. We answer questions on incoterms, documents, taxation, means of payment, licences and certificates.

What do you need to move?

Tell us origin, destination and goods. We reply with price and lead time.
