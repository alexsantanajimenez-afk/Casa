Frequently asked questions

Domestic shipping.

Answer first, detail after.

Which destinations do you ship to within Spain?

To the whole of Spain: Mainland, Balearic Islands, Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla. Also to Andorra. coverage to be confirmed

Do you ship to Andorra?

Yes. Ask us about lead times and documentation for that destination. conditions to be confirmed

Why are you specialists in the Mainland–Canary Islands corridor?

We have operated the corridor since 1992, with regular air and sea lines, customs agents and offices on the islands.

How long does a domestic shipment take?

To the Canary Islands, 24–48 h by air cargo from pick-up and from 72 h by LCL sea freight. To the Balearic Islands, 24–48 h door to door. For the rest, with the quote.

Where are you shipping to?

Tell us origin, destination and goods. We reply with price and lead time.
